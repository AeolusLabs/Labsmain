brain-points.bin is a point sample of a 3D brain mesh, resampled by AeolusLabs (scripts/bake-brain-points.mjs).

Source model: "Brain" by dgallichan (https://sketchfab.com/dgallichan)
Original: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/brain-cadd2bde67404c43b2359a6a3281d84a
Distributed via NIH 3D: "Detailed Human Brain Model (3D)", entry 3DPX-021161, v1.01, by Johnson J
https://3d.nih.gov/entries/21161/1.01
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Changes made: the mesh was converted to a random surface point sample, recentred and rescaled. No endorsement by the authors is implied.
